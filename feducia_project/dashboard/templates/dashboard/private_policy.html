Privacy Policy

At FIDUCIA, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your personal information is handled. We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. This information allows us to better understand how users interact with FIDUCIA, identify potential issues, and continuously improve the overall performance, usability, and reliability of our services. We do not intentionally collect unnecessary or excessive personal data.

Your data is used to operate and maintain your account, provide access to platform features, and ensure the security and integrity of the system. We may also use your information to personalize your experience, improve platform functionality, and develop new features.

In some cases, we may use aggregated and anonymized data for analytical purposes. This means the data cannot be used to identify you personally, but helps us improve the platform for all users.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include secure servers, encryption practices, and controlled access to sensitive data.

However, it is important to understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to use strong passwords and protect their login credentials.

4. Sharing of Information

FIDUCIA does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when it is necessary for the operation of the platform, such as hosting services, analytics tools, or security systems.

All third-party partners are required to follow strict confidentiality obligations and are only allowed to use your data for the specific purposes defined by us.

We may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences, maintain session information, and analyze general usage patterns.

You have the ability to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features within FIDUCIA.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill legal obligations. When your data is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

If you choose to delete your account, we will remove your personal information from active systems, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request restrictions on how your data is processed or object to certain types of processing.

These rights can typically be exercised through your account settings or by contacting us directly.

8. Children’s Privacy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the platform evolves. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically. Continued use of FIDUCIA after changes are made indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact
