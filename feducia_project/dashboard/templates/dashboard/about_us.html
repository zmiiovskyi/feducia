About FIDUCIA

We are a passionate team of students from the Mechanics and Mathematics Faculty of LNU. We are working hard to bring our innovative project to life, with the invaluable support and guidance of our mentor, arc-lena.

The Problem

Public speaking anxiety is the #1 social fear. According to statistics:

  • 75% of the population suffers from fear of public speaking.
  • 30% of students lose points during presentations due to anxiety.
  • This fear often leads to lost business deals and hinders career growth.

Our Solution

FIDUCIA is an AI-assistant designed specifically for public speaking. It helps you overcome anxiety and improve your skills by analyzing:

  • Body Language: Posture, gestures, and eye contact.
  • Voice: Tone, pace, and clarity.

Perfect for students, business professionals, and educators!
